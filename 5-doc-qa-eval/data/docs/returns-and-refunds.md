# Returns and Refunds

Nimbus Outdoor Co. wants you to be happy with your gear. This page explains how returns and refunds work for products bought from our online store and from Nimbus retail shops.

## Return window for gear

You can return unused gear within 30 days of the delivery date for a full refund of the purchase price. Items must be in their original condition with tags attached and must come with the original packaging. Tents, sleeping bags and packs that show signs of use, dirt or odors cannot be returned, but they may be covered by our warranty if something is wrong with them.

## Final sale items

Items marked "Final Sale" on the product page cannot be returned or exchanged. This includes clearance gear, gift cards, and custom-embroidered items. Headlamp batteries that have been opened are also final sale for hygiene and safety reasons.

## How to start a return

Sign in to your account, open the order, and choose "Start a return" to get a prepaid return label. Pack the item with the packing slip and drop it off with the carrier named on the label. If you bought in a shop, bring the item and your receipt to any Nimbus shop within the 30-day window. Return shipping is free for defective items. For all other online returns, a flat $6.95 return shipping fee is deducted from your refund.

## When you will get your money

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after our warehouse receives your return. If you choose store credit instead, it is added to your account within 2 business days and includes a 10% bonus. Refunds for gift purchases go to the gift giver unless a gift receipt is provided.

## Damaged or wrong items

If your order arrives damaged or you receive the wrong item, contact support within 14 days of delivery with a photo. We will send a replacement at no cost or refund you in full, including original shipping charges, whichever you prefer.
