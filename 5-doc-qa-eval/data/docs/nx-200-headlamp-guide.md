# NX-200 Headlamp User Guide

## Specifications

The NX-200 headlamp produces up to 400 lumens on its brightest setting. It uses a built-in 2000 mAh lithium-ion battery, weighs 92 grams, and has an IPX6 water resistance rating, which means it can handle heavy rain but should not be submerged. Charging is through a USB-C port.

## Modes and battery life

The NX-200 has a power button and a mode button. Press the power button to turn the lamp on or off. Press the mode button to cycle through the three white light levels. Hold the mode button for 2 seconds to switch to the red light, and double-press it for the strobe. Estimated runtime is 40 hours on low, 15 hours on medium, and 6 hours on high. The red light runs for up to 60 hours.

## Charging

A full charge takes about 3.5 hours using a 5V/2A USB-C charger. The indicator light glows red while charging and turns green when the battery is full. We recommend charging at room temperature. Charging is not possible below 0°C (32°F).

## Lockout mode

To stop the lamp from switching on inside your pack, press and hold both buttons for 3 seconds until the light flashes twice. Repeat the same steps to unlock it.

## Error codes and blink patterns

- Error E-4102: the battery temperature is too high. Unplug the charger, move the lamp to a cool place, and wait 30 minutes before charging again.
- Error E-4107: charging fault. Try a different cable and charger. If the code returns, contact support for a warranty check.
- Three red blinks at start-up: the battery is below 10% and needs charging.

## Resetting the lamp

If the lamp does not respond, hold the power button for 10 seconds to perform a hard reset. Your saved settings will return to factory defaults.
