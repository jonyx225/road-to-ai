# Shipping and Delivery

## Shipping options and speed

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days within the contiguous United States and costs $5.95. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $14.95. Orders placed before 2 p.m. Mountain Time on a business day ship the same day. Orders over $75 qualify for free standard shipping.

## Alaska, Hawaii and territories

Delivery to Alaska, Hawaii, and US territories takes 6 to 10 business days by standard shipping, and express shipping is not available. A $9.95 surcharge applies to all orders to these destinations, and the free shipping threshold for them is $150.

## International shipping

We ship to Canada and the United Kingdom. Canadian orders usually arrive in 7 to 12 business days and UK orders in 10 to 15 business days. Customers are responsible for import duties and taxes collected by the carrier at delivery. We cannot ship stoves, fuel canisters, or bear spray outside the United States.

## Tracking your order

You will receive an email with a tracking number when your order ships. Tracking updates can take up to 24 hours to appear. You can also follow your order from the Orders page in your account.

## Lost or delayed packages

If your tracking has not updated for 5 business days, or your package has not arrived 10 business days after the ship date, contact support and we will open a carrier investigation. Once the package is confirmed lost, we will ship a replacement or refund you, your choice. We are not able to reroute packages that are already in transit, but you can change the delivery address within 2 hours of placing an order.
