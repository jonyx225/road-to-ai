# Account and Security

## Creating a strong password

Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and cannot be one of the 10,000 most common passwords. We recommend using a password manager. We never ask for your password by email, phone or chat.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication (2FA) is optional but strongly recommended. You can use an authenticator app or text messages. When you turn on 2FA, we give you 10 one-time backup codes. Store them somewhere safe, because each one can be used once if you lose your phone.

## Locked accounts and password resets

After 5 failed sign-in attempts, your account is locked for 15 minutes. You can reset your password from the sign-in page at any time, and the reset link is valid for 60 minutes. If you no longer have access to your email address, contact support and be ready to verify your identity with your most recent order number.

## Contacting support

Support is available Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. Mountain Time, by live chat, by email at support@nimbus-outdoor.example, or by phone at 1-800-555-0142. Email replies typically arrive within 1 business day. We are closed on major US holidays.

## Data deletion and privacy

You can ask us to delete your account and personal data from Account > Privacy, or by contacting support. Requests are completed within 30 days. We must keep order and invoice records for 7 years for tax purposes, but we remove your name from marketing lists immediately. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link in each email.
