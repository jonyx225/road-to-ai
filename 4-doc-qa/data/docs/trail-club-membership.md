# Nimbus Trail Club Membership

## Plans and pricing

The Trail Club costs $12 per month or $110 per year. Both plans include 10% off all gear, free standard shipping on every order with no minimum, and early access to seasonal sales. New members get a $15 welcome credit after the first paid billing date.

## Billing and renewal

Memberships renew automatically on the same day of the month (or year) you joined. We charge the card on file and send a receipt by email. If a payment fails, we retry after 3 days and again after 5 days. If it is still unsuccessful, there is a 7-day grace period, after which the membership is suspended until the payment method is updated.

## Cancelling a membership

You can cancel any time from Account > Membership. Benefits continue until the end of the period you already paid for. Cancelling does not delete your account or your order history.

## Refunds for membership fees

Monthly fees are not refundable once the billing date has passed. If you were charged for an annual renewal by mistake, we will refund the annual fee in full if you ask within 14 days of the renewal charge. After 14 days, annual fees are not refunded, but you keep the benefits until the term ends.

## Pausing your membership

You can pause your membership for up to 3 months once per calendar year. While paused, you are not billed and member discounts do not apply. Your membership resumes automatically when the pause ends.
