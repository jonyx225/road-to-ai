# Warranty and Repairs

## What our warranty covers

Nimbus products are covered against defects in materials and workmanship. The length of coverage depends on the product category, as described below. The warranty applies to the original purchaser and starts on the delivery date.

## Coverage by product type

- Tents and backpacks: 5 years.
- Sleeping bags and insulated jackets: 3 years.
- Electronics such as headlamps and chargers: 2 years.
- Rechargeable batteries: 1 year or 300 charge cycles, whichever comes first.
- Accessories such as stuff sacks, straps and bottles: 90 days.

## What is not covered

Normal wear and tear, damage from misuse, accidents, unauthorized modifications, and damage caused by improper storage (such as storing a wet tent) are not covered. Fading from sunlight and small scratches that do not affect performance are also excluded.

## Making a warranty claim

Email warranty@nimbus-outdoor.example with your order number and photos of the problem. We reply within 2 business days. If the claim is approved, we will repair the item or replace it with the same or a comparable product. We pay shipping both ways for approved warranty claims.

## Out-of-warranty repairs

We repair most gear even when the warranty has ended. Zipper replacement costs a flat $25. Tent pole replacement costs a flat $40 for a single pole section. Sewing repairs are quoted after inspection. Repairs typically take 10 to 15 business days plus shipping time.
